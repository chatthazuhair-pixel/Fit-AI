# FitAI

A practical fitness companion for beginners who want to build a consistent at-home routine with workouts that fit their time, experience, and available equipment.

**Try FitAI:** https://fit-jgqg52cra-chatthazuhair-4141.vercel.app

## What you can do
- Create a simple profile to personalize general fitness guidance.
- Choose a short or longer at-home workout.
- Follow a guided exercise sequence and track completed sessions.
- Build meal entries by meal type (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner or Snack).
- Search an international food catalogue and choose standard servings, bowls or plate portions without entering gram quantities.
- View estimated nutrition for the selected portion; the app stores the underlying reference weight for calculation.
- Follow timed workout sessions with warm-up, movement intervals, built-in recovery and cool-down.
- Download a formatted PDF report or a JSON data backup, and delete the data associated with your browser ID.
- Ask the AI coach for general fitness and healthy-habit guidance.

## Food catalogue
- Search 80+ internationally familiar foods across grains, protein, fish, dairy alternatives, fruit, vegetables, legumes, nuts, seeds, and oils.
- Choose a standard serving, half/double serving, bowl or plate guide. The standard weight in grams is shown as an estimate; users do not need to enter grams manually.
- Meal entries can be grouped into Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and Snack.
- The backend calculates nutrition from the exact catalogue item and its reference weight. Plate and bowl sizes are guides, not universal measurements.
- Generic nutrition values are approximate reference estimates informed by USDA FoodData Central; this is not a live USDA database connection. Each label identifies the preparation state. Brands, recipes, cooking methods and portions can change actual values.
- USDA FoodData Central: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/

## Important notes
- FitAI provides general information, not medical advice or a substitute for a qualified professional.
- Nutrition values and portion estimates are approximate; homemade recipes and brands vary.
- Workout plans use time-based movement blocks, warm-up, recovery and cool-down. They are general activity guidance rather than coaching prescriptions or performance targets.
- If you are under 18, the coach avoids weight-loss targets and restrictive eating advice.
- FitAI currently uses an anonymous browser ID, not verified account authentication. Clearing browser storage may make that ID inaccessible; do not store sensitive information.
- The public version's data persistence depends on its hosting configuration. Do not use it to store sensitive information.
